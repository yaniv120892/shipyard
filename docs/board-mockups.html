My recommendation: B, with C as a toggle later

Your answer to question 1 was D — ship runs plus ambient PRs, Linear tasks, and plain branches. Kanban's columns only mean something for /ship runs; half your card types would live in catch-all columns, which erodes the layout's one advantage. The card grid gives every source a first-class card, puts "needs you" at the top unconditionally, and each ship card still shows its pipeline position on the progress strip.

If the board ever becomes /ship-only, A is the better fit — nothing in the data model prevents adding it as a view mode later. C is worth adding as a compact toggle once you regularly run 8+ parallel flows.
